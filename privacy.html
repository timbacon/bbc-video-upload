Privacy policy: BBC Video Upload

Last updated: 18 November 2026

BBC Video Upload is an internal tool for Baptist Bible Church Zim that uploads the church's Sunday service recordings to the church's own YouTube channel. It uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

What it accesses

  • Upload videos (YouTube permission youtube.upload): to upload the church's videos and set their thumbnails.
  • View the YouTube account (YouTube permission youtube.readonly): only to show the channel's name and whether it can take long videos, so the person running it can check they are uploading to the right channel.

It does not read, change or delete any other videos, comments, subscribers or account information.

What it stores

Only the sign-in (an OAuth refresh token), saved on the single computer that runs the tool, so it does not have to sign in every time. It also keeps a note of the IDs of the videos it has uploaded, on the same computer. Nothing is sent anywhere except to YouTube, and no data is kept on any server of ours.

What it shares

Nothing. No data is sold, shared with or shown to anyone else. Videos go up private; the church publishes them itself in YouTube Studio.

Withdrawing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time from the Google account's security settings: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. The saved sign-in can also be deleted on the computer that runs the tool.
