BBC Video Upload

BBC Video Upload is a small internal tool for Baptist Bible Church Zim. It uploads the church's own Sunday service recordings (the sermon and the worship service) to the church's YouTube channel.

It is run by hand by the church's volunteer video editor, on one computer. Videos are uploaded as private, with their titles, chapters and thumbnails, and a person checks each one and publishes it in YouTube Studio.

It is not a public service: nobody else can sign in to it, and it does not read, store or share anyone else's data.

This tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service.
